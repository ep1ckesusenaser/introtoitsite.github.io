Network detective

Notes for Lab 8a. Here is what I found out about the campus network.

Clue #1 · ISP

Uzbektelecom AS8193

On the campus network my internet provider is Uzbektelecom, autonomous system AS8193.

Clue #2 · Campus gateway

Fortinet

The gateway that connects the campus network to the internet was made by Fortinet.

Clue #3 · Distance to inha.uz

6 hops

inha.uz is six hops away from my computer. A hop is one jump a packet makes from one network node to the next.
